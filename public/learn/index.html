Welcome

Learn the SAT one part at a time.

From Aime Pascal, your instructor

When I was studying, the SAT became easier the day I started to notice its mind map. It is not one big test. It is a few parts, each part has smaller parts, and each small part has its own rules.

So do not try to learn everything at once. Go part by part, and finish one before you open the next. If you already know what hurts your score, go straight to that part.

The SAT map

Parts, sub-parts and skills

The SAT has two parts. Each part has four sub-parts (domains). Each domain has skills. The lines around the boxes tell you which domain a skill belongs to.
